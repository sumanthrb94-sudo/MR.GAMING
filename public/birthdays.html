Terms & conditions Booking, guests, time, food, safety and cancellation

Booking & payment

  1. Your date and time are confirmed only after the ₹4,000 advance is received.
  2. The balance is paid on the day of the party, at the venue.
  3. We accept UPI, cash and card.
  4. Prices: ₹675 per child and ₹399 per adult for the 3-hour package. Minimum charge is for 10 guests.

Guests

  1. Please confirm your final guest count at least 2 days before the party. We bill the confirmed count, or the guests who attend if that is more.
  2. Extra guests on the day are added at the same per-guest rates, as long as we can serve them.
  3. Up to 15 guests: the game room is exclusively yours. More than 15: the whole restaurant is yours.

Time

  1. The package runs for 3 hours from the booked start time. A late start does not extend the end time.
  2. Extra time can be added only if the room is free; please ask us for the rate.
  3. All parties finish by 11 pm.

Food

  1. We are 100% pure veg. Every guest gets the food listed in the package; adults get paneer pizza instead of Margherita.
  2. Outside food and drinks are not allowed, except your birthday cake.
  3. Please tell us about any food allergies when you confirm the guest count.

Games & safety

  1. A parent or guardian must stay with the children for the whole party.
  2. Please handle consoles, controllers, board games and furniture with care. Damage is charged at repair or replacement cost.
  3. Our staff may pause games if equipment is being misused.

General

  1. Bookings and changes are made by call or WhatsApp on +91 73372 30311.
  2. Quotes are for the package and guest count shown on them.

Cancellation & change policy

  1. You cancel: a ₹1,000 cancellation charge applies. We keep ₹1,000 of the ₹4,000 advance and refund ₹3,000.
  2. You change the date: one change is free if you ask at least 3 days before the party, subject to availability. Your advance moves to the new date.
  3. No-show: if no one arrives within the booked time, the advance is not refunded.
  4. We cancel: you get the full advance back or a new date of your choice.
  5. Refunds go back to the same UPI account or the payment method you used.
